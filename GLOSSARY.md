# Glossary

**Artwork**
A movie or a TV show known to the app. Shown on its own detail page (Movie or Show).

**Gallery**
The page listing all the images TMDB holds for one Artwork: its backdrops and its posters, mixed in a single list. For a show, it covers the show itself, never its seasons or episodes.

**Backdrop**
A horizontal TMDB image of an Artwork.

**Poster**
A vertical TMDB image of an Artwork.

**Artwork image**
The image displayed at the top of an Artwork's page. Opens the Gallery when tapped.
