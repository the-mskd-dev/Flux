Flux is a modern, 100% open-source media library and video player for Android, built to organize your local movies, TV shows, and anime. It scans your offline video files and fetches rich metadata (posters, synopses, episode details, genres, and ratings) from The Movie Database (TMDB).

This F-Droid build is completely free, ad-free, tracker-free, and contains zero proprietary Google dependencies.

### 🎬 Key Features

- 📂 Movies, Shows & Anime Library: Organize your offline film and series collection into a modern, easy-to-browse catalog.
- 🍿 TMDB Metadata Sync: Automatic posters, plot summaries, episode tracking, and ratings.
- ▶️ Built-in & External Players: Smooth video playback with progress history or launch in your favorite external player.
- 🛡️ Pure FOSS: No ads, no telemetry, no proprietary SDKs, and no account required.
